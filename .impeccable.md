## Design Context
Users: a nontechnical cannabis branding agency founder researching prospects and managing relationships.
Purpose: traceable research, evidence-based opportunity assessment, and a human-led CRM.
Personality: clear, deliberate, professional. Compact operational workspace, not a marketing site.
Design: Bootstrap 5.3 foundation, cool neutrals, forest green accent, system sans for reliable Windows rendering. Both themes. Variation 3, motion 1, density 7. Radius 6px for controls, 12px for panels. No entry or navigation animations.
Principles: evidence before conclusions; human control; honest unknowns; accessible controls; durable exports.
